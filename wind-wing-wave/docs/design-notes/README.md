# Design notes (background reading)

These are the independent design documents and critiques written before the build, kept so
future sessions can see where ideas came from. **They are not the spec.** Where they disagree with
`../PLAN.md`, `../ARCHITECTURE.md` or `../DECISIONS.md`, those win.

- `ecology.md`: the life system, the arrival model and the full real-species research catalogue, with sources.
- `player-experience.md`: the player's journey, tools, rewards, journal and storms.
- `look-and-sound.md`: materials, plants, animals, arrival scenes, sky and the sound recipes.
- `architecture-draft.md`: the first technical draft. Note that the final architecture uses GPU-displaced terrain instead of surface-net tiles.
- `critique-*.md`: three critiques (the owner's advocate, fun, phone feasibility) that settled the contradictions.
